Everything a party needs

📀 CDG & LRC formats

Plays classic MP3+CDG karaoke discs pixel-for-pixel, plus LRC lyric files with word-by-word highlighting.

🎯 Singing score

Turn on the mic and the app compares your pitch to the backing track live — streaks, confetti, and a final score.

✨ Themed parties

Christmas, Halloween, Neon Party, Kids and more — each with its own look, particles, and vibe. Or build your own theme.

📁 Folder import

Point it at your karaoke collection and it pairs MP3s with CDG/LRC files and auto-categorizes by title.

🔌 Works offline

Your library lives in the browser's own storage. After the first load, no internet required.

🔒 Private by design

No accounts, no uploads, no analytics. Everything — songs, scores, settings — stays on your device.

🐳 Self-hosted

Run it on a home server or NAS as the house party box.

curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/retroverse-studios/karaoke-stage/main/docker-compose.yml
docker compose up -d

Pulls ghcr.io/retroverse-studios/karaoke-stage. Mic scoring needs HTTPS (or localhost) — see the compose file notes.
